1.What it is, in one paragraph

The author hands the machine an arrangement (or a story seed, or a brain-dump). The machine plans the book — premise, structure, characters, world, outline, voice — pausing twice for the author to catch wrong turns. Then it drafts the book one piece at a time, fully autonomously, stopping only for problems serious enough to need a human. When the draft is done, the Cleanup Crew flags book-scale problems for the author to approve.

It's a sanctioned fork of Elizabeth Ann West's Novel Writing Machine v0.2. The donor's three-phase architecture is kept; the metaphor (F1 racing → cozy crime family), the autonomy model, the planning layer, series support, pen-name voice, and the editing roster are changed. The Family runs itself between sit-downs and only kicks the big calls upstairs to the Boss.

Decision fatigue is the enemy. When in doubt about whether something needs the author, it does not. — Operating constraint baked into the Boss

3.The three phases

The Setup  ·  planning

All the work before a single word of prose is written.

An arrangement comes in via one of three on-ramps. The machine builds eight planning artifacts: premise, beat sheet (against the named framework), scene outline, character interiority, world + the Books, style sheet, conflict map, dialogue cards. The author ratifies twice — once after the foundation, once after the blueprint.

Gate 1 — Foundation Gate 2 — Blueprint Optional shakedown piece

The Job  ·  drafting

One piece at a time, strict outline order, halt only on real breaks.

The Wheelman runs the seven-step loop on every piece. The Crew (Fixer / Bookkeeper / Earpiece) preps in parallel. The Consiglieres audit. The Boss applies the severity ladder. Sit-downs settle on the Boss's own authority. Only "Do it over" and "Kick it upstairs" halt. The one mid-draft creative call surfaced to the author is drift = discovery.

Strict in-order drafting Freeze on halt No permission pings

The Cleanup  ·  post-draft inspection

Six inspectors catch book-scale problems no single-piece reader can see.

The Closer (manuscript-scope craft), the Wire (rhythm), the Tail (crutch frequency + the author's editing roster), the Alibi (pacing-vs-plan), the Cold Read (engagement), the Payoff (promises kept). All flag candidates to one file; the author ratifies per flag. voice-preservation runs as the do-not-cut counterweight. For series books, propose-at-close drafts bible updates. Final pass: proofreader.

One craft authority (§11.2) Nothing auto-applies Series-bible propose-at-close

4.The five human moments

Per §12 of the spec — these are the only times the machine kicks something upstairs to the author. Everything else runs autonomously.

Intake review

Approve or correct the arrangement (and, for series books, the auto-seeded fields from the bible's Read-at-Open).

Gate 1 — Foundation

Premise + structure + characters before the rest of planning. If wrong here, everything downstream is wrong.

Gate 2 — Blueprint

World, Books, outline, voice anchor, conflict map, dialogue cards. The donor's "scrutineering" gate. Author signs off; the Setup closes.

Drift = discovery

The one mid-draft creative call — only fires when the prose discovers something better than the outline planned. Series-impact discoveries ride this gate too.

Cleanup ratification

The author works through every flag. Accept / reject / revise. Series-bible propose-at-close ratifies here too if applicable.

5.The §3 severity ladder — the autonomy spine

The single most important behavior rule in the fork. It's what buys back the author's evenings.

2WordQueued for next checkpoint. Piece counts provisionally. Continue.No
3Sit-downBoss settles on his own authority. Makes the call, applies it, logs it. Continue.No
4Do it overPiece was wrong; redraft with the issue as a constraint. Old draft discarded (saved to the Paper Trail).Yes

Four jurisdictions on every finished piece — Bookkeeper's (continuity + series canon), Voice (with voice-bleed guard), Family (character truth), Rhythm (pace).

The Cleanup Crew

Manuscript-scope craft

the Closer

Wraps craft-editor at book scope. Pre-runs voice-preservation. Routes every other inspector's ratified rewrites through craft-editor.

Cadence

the Wire

Sentence rhythm + opener variety across chapters. Calibrated to this pen name's voice anchor.

Frequency + roster

the Tail

Crutch-list frequency/density + orchestrates the author's five editing skills + voice-preservation as do-not-cut counterweight.

Timeline holds up

the Alibi

Tension + word counts vs. the plan. Drag zones, runaway zones, thread-cadence misses.

Reader simulation

the Cold Read

Sequential first-read engagement curve. Confused / bored / hooked / moved / suspicious — the curve matters more than any single moment.

Promises kept

the Payoff

Broken promises, orphan payoffs, partial landings. Cross-references the Cold Read's engagement at planned landings.

The process record

Keeping receipts

the Paper Trail

Every real run leaves a timestamped folder on disk — prompts, outputs, calls, ratifications, saved artifacts. Nothing the machine does is invisible.

Dry-run toggle

If .mock-mode exists at project root, every model call is replaced with placeholder content. Prove the wiring works before spending a cent.

9.The three identity levels

Voice lives at the pen-name level and is inherited by every book under that pen name. There is no per-book voice override.

PEN NAME   — voice identity (voice bible, style guide, voice anchor,
              crutch list, prose rules, audience profile)
  └─ SERIES   — story-world identity (world, recurring cast,
                  meta-arc, series canon)   [OPTIONAL]
       └─ BOOK   — the work (the-arrangement, planning stack,
                manuscript, the Books, runs/)

A standalone book skips the Series level but still names a pen name. A series belongs to exactly one pen name. The folder location is the ground truth; the arrangement's identity fields point into it (§6.5).

12.The 14 build steps

  1. Project skeleton + the Paper Trail + mock-mode. Foundation — folders, run-evidence convention, mock-mode toggle. Built first so every later piece could be tested without spending API credit.
  2. Reuse donor KEEP skills. Copied 9 donor skills as-is (one renamed: world-engineer → the Books). Caught and fixed a Windows-cp1252 bug in the Books' validator.
  3. Fork Driver → the Wheelman. Same loop wrapper, mafia naming, mock-mode and Paper-Trail checks added.
  4. Build the-arrangement.md template. Forked book-order.md with four new fields (pen_name, series_name, book_number, structure_framework).
  5. Pen-name/series/book hierarchy + the Pedigree. Six pen-name templates + the resolver skill + the Python script that walks the tree.
  6. series-bible.md modular template + the series-bible skill. Read-at-open auto-seeds; propose-at-close drafts updates for ratification.
  7. Swappable beat-framework machinery + Romancing the Beat. Full RtB recipe card; three stubs.
  8. Conflict Map + Dialogue Cards planning artifacts. Two NEW named artifacts that the donor folded elsewhere.
  9. Fork the Crew + the Consiglieres. Three Crew sub-roles as separate skills; four Consigliere jurisdictions in one skill. Series-canon + voice-bleed wirings.
  10. Fork the Cleanup Crew. Six inspectors. Three were donor TODOs that got real designs.
  11. Wire the Tail's editing roster. Copied 12 anthropic-skills in. audience-audit rewritten pen-name-aware.
  12. Fork team-principal → the Boss. The keystone. §3 ladder, freeze-on-halt, two planning gates, no permission pings.
  13. Wire three intake on-ramps. Filled / Seedsmith semantic mapping / brain-dump interview.
  14. End-to-end mock-mode smoke test. 11 phases, all passed. No API credit spent.

13.How to start a real book

  1. Open Claude Code in D:\code\the-backroom\. Tell it: "I want to start a new book." The Boss takes it from there.
  2. Bring your pen-name voice files when you create that pen name. Drop your existing voice bible / style guide / Rachel Morgan audience profile straight into the pen-name folder — don't re-write what you've already done.
  3. First real run should stay in mock mode. Create .mock-mode at project root, walk through one full piece end-to-end, watch what files write where and how the gates surface. Then delete the flag.
  4. Tune Romancing the Beat against your first romance. The recipe card is honest about being v1; the spec expects you to refine it against your first real book through the machine.
